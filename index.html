About

I believe economic justice means everyone, no matter their birthplace, should have the opportunity to thrive, not just escape poverty. And history shows that improving the wellbeing of the world's poor is best achieved through broad-based economic growth.

That's why I co-founded Growth Teams. We partner with governments in developing countries to translate pro-growth policies into concrete actions that unlock industries, create jobs, and drive lasting economic progress.

Over the past decade, my career has spanned impact investing in India, advising government leaders on industrialization in Africa, and managing large-scale impact evaluations. Throughout it all, I've been driven by a passion for expanding opportunity.

Born in Rochester, NY. Based in Cape Town, South Africa. Fan of both footballs — Hala Madrid and Go Bills.
